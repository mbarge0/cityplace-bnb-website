How we turn a suite

  • Every suite is professionally cleaned between stays — full linen change, disinfected bathroom, clean kitchen, mopped floors, restocked supplies.
  • Laundry is done at a professional laundromat.
  • After the clean, we photograph the suite with TimePhoto. The app stamps a verified date, time, and address directly on every image.
  • Photos are taken before check-in and retained as a record of condition at the start of every stay.
  • We keep a regular pest-control plan on the property.
