Operations Summary

The systems, the staff, and what transfers at close

The reason to look at CityPlace bnb is not the building. It is that the operation running the building transfers with it — the property management system, the pricing engine, the reservation manager, the cleaning bench, the vendors, and the documentation that ties them together.

Ten suites at 3604 San Jacinto Street have been run for roughly four years without an on-site manager. The owner has not lived in Dallas at any point during that period. What makes that possible is the stack set out on the next page, and every piece of it is included in a sale.

01How the week actually runs

The manager holds the front line

Every guest message, across every channel, goes to the reservation manager. She has held the role for four years, knows the building and the guest mix, and orders supplies without being asked. The owner is not in the inbox.

Pricing runs itself

RoomPriceGenie moves nightly rates against demand and the competitive set inside Cloudbeds. Rates are not set by hand.

Turnovers are a bench, not a person

A lead cleaner works from documented checklists, with backups on standing call. Smart locks mean no key handoff, and Ring cameras at each unit give an entry record when a dispute needs one.

What is left is ownership

Roughly five hours a week: reviewing pricing and occupancy, approving spend, and handling exceptions in the Slack channel where the whole team already coordinates.

02Transition

The seller offers an optional two-month transition support contract.

Available at the buyer’s election, to hand over the stack, the vendor relationships and the operating rhythm rather than leaving the buyer to reconstruct them. Terms are negotiable as part of the transaction.

05Demand

Where the guests come from

Baylor medical district

A two-minute walk to Baylor University Medical Center, a 914-bed Level I trauma center. Families of patients, traveling clinicians and visiting specialists are the anchor demand, and it does not follow a leisure calendar.

Corporate housing

Full kitchens and 550 sq ft suites support 30-plus-day stays. Stays of 30 days or longer are exempt from Texas hotel occupancy tax, which changes the economics of that segment materially.

Downtown, Deep Ellum, Uptown

Leisure and business travel into central Dallas fills around the medical and corporate base. Free parking, pet-friendly policy and a private dog park differentiate the property against downtown hotel inventory on both price and practicality.

06Regulatory

Short-term rental regulation in Dallas

Dallas passed short-term rental ordinances in 2023. Those ordinances have been the subject of ongoing litigation, and enforcement has been repeatedly challenged in court. Separately, the property won its own platform appeal on all four points in February 2025.

This is stated as a matter of record, not as a legal opinion or an assurance of any particular outcome. The regulatory position is a diligence item, and buyers should evaluate it with their own counsel. It is also worth noting that a meaningful portion of the property’s demand comes from 30-plus-day corporate and medical stays, which sit outside short-term rental frameworks entirely.

07Scope

What is included in a sale

  1. The real estate. The building and land at 3604 San Jacinto Street, Dallas, Texas 75204 — ten renovated 1BR/1BA suites, private dog park, and parking.
  2. All furnishings and guest items. The units convey furnished and operating, down to the kitchen inventory. No re-fit is required to keep taking reservations.
  3. The technology stack. Cloudbeds, RoomPriceGenie, smart locks and code logs, Ring cameras, and the Slack operations channel.
  4. Systems documentation. Checklists, standard operating procedures and training materials as they are used today.
  5. Staff and vendor relationships. Introductions to the reservation manager, lead cleaner and backup bench, and handyman. These are independent relationships; the seller introduces them, and the buyer contracts with them directly.
