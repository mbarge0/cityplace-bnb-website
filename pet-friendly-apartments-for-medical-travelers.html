Pet-Friendly Apartments for Medical Travelers

The short answer

CityPlace bnb has ten pet-friendly, furnished one-bedroom suites at 3604 San Jacinto Street in Dallas. The property is approximately a two-minute walk from Baylor University Medical Center. Each suite includes a full kitchen and dedicated workspace, while contactless check-in supports extended stays. Travelers with dogs also have access to an on-property dog park. Free private off-street parking is available for guests who bring a vehicle.
